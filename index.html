About this portal

This portal lets students of the Civil Engineering Department look up the attendance of all courses by roll number. Data comes from the official attendance sheet for the 1st Year 1st Semester Examination-2026.

Attendance marks. Every course carries 10 marks for attendance. The portal estimates them as attended ÷ conducted × 10, rounded to the nearest whole number (maximum 10). The total is shown out of 80 (8 courses × 10).

The data was digitised from a scanned copy, so a few figures may be misread. If something looks wrong, please check it against the official sheet in the department office.
